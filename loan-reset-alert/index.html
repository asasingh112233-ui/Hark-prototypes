What's happening

RBI raised rates, and banks are following.

On 7 Oct 2026 the RBI raised the repo rate by 0.25% to 5.50%, its first hike in this cycle. Banks are now raising their lending rates one by one, so many borrowers' EMIs will quietly go up at their next reset.

The jargon, in plain words.

Repo rate

The rate RBI lends to banks at. When it goes up, loans get costlier.

MCLR

A bank's own minimum lending rate. Loans linked to it change only on a reset date, usually every 6 months, 1 year or 2 years from when the loan started.

EBLR / RLLR (repo-linked rate)

Moves with the RBI repo rate. Banks must reset these at least once every 3 months, so changes reach you fast.

Fixed rate

Doesn't change. The bank can't raise it.
